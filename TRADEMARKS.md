# Fenmyr's name, Myra's character and art

Fenmyr's **code** is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use it, change it, share it and build on it.

The **brand** is different. These belong to Stephen Chiang and are **all rights reserved**. They are not covered by the code license:

- The name **"Fenmyr"** for this app, and the Fenmyr logo and app icon (`assets/`, `tools/make-icon.ts`)
- The name **"Myra"** for the assistant, and **Myra**, the cloudberry character, in any form: her drawing in the code (`src/web/goo.ts`), her stickers (`web/stamps/myra-*`), and any art of her
- Fenmyr's and Myra's own art and illustrations: the stamp set (`web/stamps/`), the cover and other images in `assets/`
- Their own icons, which are the cloudberry mark, the Myra tool and the chat icon (`tools/icons/custom.ts`), and the cloudberry glyph font (`web/fonts/MyraMark.woff2`). The rest of the icons are Phosphor Icons (MIT), which keep their own license, listed in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

## If you make your own version

You're welcome to build on the code. If you share or publish your version:

- **Give it a different name**, and don't present it as Fenmyr or as made by Stephen Chiang.
- **Take Myra out**, along with the stamps, the app icon, the cloudberry mark, the other icons of ours and the art, and use your own.
- Keep the copyright and license notices from [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Talking about Fenmyr or Myra (reviews, tutorials, "I made this with Fenmyr") is always fine.
