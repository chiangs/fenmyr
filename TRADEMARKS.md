Myra's **code** is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE): use it, learn from it, change it, share it, build your own thing with it.

The **brand** is different. These belong to Stephen Chiang and are **all rights reserved**. They are not covered by the code license:

- The name **"Myra"** for this app, and the Myra logo and app icon (`assets/`)
- **Myra**, the cloudberry character, in any form: her drawing in the code (`web/goo.js`), her stickers (`web/stamps/myra-*`), and any art of her
- Myra's own art and illustrations: the stamp set (`web/stamps/`), the cover and other images in `assets/`
- Myra's own icons: the cloudberry mark, the Myra tool and the chat icon (`tools/icons/custom.js`), and the cloudberry glyph font (`web/fonts/MyraMark.woff2`). The rest of the icons are Phosphor Icons (MIT), which keep their own license, listed in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

## If you make your own version

You're welcome to build on the code. If you share or publish your version:

- **Give it a different name**, and don't present it as Myra or as made by Stephen Chiang.
- **Take Myra out**, along with the stamps, the app icon, Myra's own icons and the art, and use your own.
- Keep the copyright and license notices from [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Talking about Myra (reviews, tutorials, "I made this with Myra") is always fine.

## Where Myra came from

Myra grew from [Mochiboard](https://github.com/jessawolfe-ux/mochiboard) by [Jessa Wolfe](https://jessa.art), whose code is MIT licensed. Mochiboard's name, Mochi (its pixel goo character), its stamps, art and music belong to Jessa Wolfe. They are not part of Myra and Myra doesn't use them.
